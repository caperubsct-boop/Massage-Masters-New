FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp at 050 203 2707.

How do I book?

Send a WhatsApp message to 050 203 2707 with the treatment, duration, date, time and whether you would like the studio or a mobile visit. We confirm quickly, day or night. You can also use the booking builder to write the message for you.

Is Massage Masters gay-friendly?

Completely. We were built for gay and bi men and welcome everyone: gay, bi, trans, straight or curious. Our male therapists are respectful and non-judgemental, and same-sex couples can book side-by-side sessions.

Are your therapists male?

Yes. All Massage Masters therapists are professional male massage therapists.

What are your prices?

Swedish starts at R900 for 60 minutes. Deep tissue from R1,050, sports from R1,250, VIP Signature from R1,000, VIP Signature from R1,400, Couples Massage from R2,600 and couples from R2,600. See the Treatments & Prices page for every duration.

Is there an extra charge for mobile or hotel massage?

A flat R200 outcall fee applies to visits to your hotel, home or office. Late-night, very early or long-distance bookings may carry custom rates, which we always confirm before we travel.

Can you come to my hotel?

Yes. We visit guests at most Cape Town hotels and guesthouses. Most hotels register visitors, so let reception know you are expecting your massage therapist. See our Hotels page for the hotels we visit most.

Which areas do you travel to?

All of greater Cape Town, including De Waterkant, Green Point, Sea Point, Camps Bay, Clifton, the V&A Waterfront, the City Bowl, the Southern and Northern Suburbs and airport hotels. See our Areas page.

Are you really open 24/7?

Yes. We take bookings 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Late-night and mobile sessions are available, and custom rates may apply. WhatsApp us for a quote.

Is it discreet?

Yes. We never share client details, hotel visits are low-key, and our WhatsApp conversations stay private.

What should I wear?

Whatever is comfortable. You will be properly draped with a clean towel throughout, and only the area being worked on is uncovered.

How do I pay?

We accept cash, card and EFT. Payment is made after your session unless a deposit is requested for late-night or long-distance bookings.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Of course. Please let us know on WhatsApp as early as possible, ideally at least 2 hours before your appointment, so we can offer the slot to another client.

Is there an age limit?

Our services are for adults aged 18 and over.

Still have a question?

We reply on WhatsApp day and night, usually within minutes.
